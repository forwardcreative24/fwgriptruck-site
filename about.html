About

Professional production equipment in a small production town.

Fort Wayne Grip Truck is your connection to professional production equipment in a small production town. The base truck kit includes what you’d expect from a typical 1-ton package, along with lighting and support items you’ll actually use. You can completely knock out a small-medium sized job with it, or use it as a reliable foundation for any shoot.

Founded by Brian Myers, a Commercial Cinematographer and the owner of Forward Creative, Fort Wayne Grip Truck is curated with the equipment Brian uses and is continuously optimized to make his life (and yours) easier.

Do you need to crew up, too? We know the local scene and are happy to refer you to some of our favorite people for key positions.
