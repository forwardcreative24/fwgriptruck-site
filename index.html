What the truck?

Fort Wayne Grip Truck makes shooting in Northeast Indiana easy.

Built by a DP who works nationally but grew up local, you get production-fluent communication, recognizable tools, and a G&E package built for the kind of days lean crews actually run — when the team is small and the schedule is tight.

If you’re coming into town for a branded doc, corporate that needs to look not corporate, or a tight commercial day and wondering, “Wait… is anything in Fort Wayne?”

Yes.

Leave the heavy stuff at home — we’ve got you covered.

From the field

FAQ’s

What if I don’t have any insurance?

You have to have rented equipment and general liability insurance to book the truck.

Details are on the insurance page.

Can I rent gear without booking the truck?

Sometimes. Gear-only rentals (no truck) have a $495/day minimum and need the same rental and liability insurance as the truck. If you just need a couple of stands and a sandbag, we’re not the right fit. If you need a real package, we can pull it off the truck for you. Email rent@fwgriptruck.com with what you need and your dates.
